Artist bio

Garfield Discoteque is the DJ alias of Leonardo Velázquez, a Mexican designer based in Vancouver. His sets move between the swing of disco, the darker edges of indie dance and the steady pulse of house and techno. He has played across Mexico, Canada and Spain, from Hello Goodbye in Vancouver to Bass Club Madrid.

In Vancouver, his work on a party often starts with the poster. Alongside his residency at Therapy Sound, he designs weekly artwork for Lullaby, helps book artists and plays the nights he promotes. His back-to-back sets with Llugcha grew out of sharing booths and playing through sunrise. At Tempo in Mexico, the two took over the booth for an entire night.
